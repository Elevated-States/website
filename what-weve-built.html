The host, the network, and what's already in motion.

A host the community already knows

◆A recognized name. Shane Mauss's episodes of Comedy Central's Tales From The Trip, the documentary Psychonautics, and his new special TRIPS have made him a familiar figure in the psychedelic space.
◆A 111-city psychedelic-science tour (2016–17), in partnership with MAPS. Shane has been networking with organizations and groups since 2015, when he first had psychedelic researchers on his podcast.
◆A 150-city tour (2023–25), built by partnering with a local organization in every city — the same city-to-city playbook this project runs on.
◆A built-in megaphone. A long-running science podcast plus regular guest spots on major podcasts across the space — audiences already primed for this work.
◆Relationships across the movement. Longstanding ties to the Zendo Project, DanceSafe, Students for Sensible Drug Policy, and more — the organizations that define credible harm reduction.
◆Real access to academia. Direct lines to researchers and clinicians — which lets us book guests you won't find on any other psychedelic speaker series.

What the project has already built

The Policy Atlas — an interactive map of all 50 states with five leaderboards.
A 463-organization resource directory — reform, harm-reduction, research, and community groups nationwide.
A 397-clinic ketamine-access directory — verified in-person providers coast to coast.
A touring event pipeline — Asheville (Apr 2027), then Boca Raton and Bloomington.
A social-media engine bringing policy and reform news from every state (launching Nov 1).
A partnership with The Pearl Institute (Asheville) and Carolinas for Care — who will help promote, run, organize, and ensure the success of our first Ball.

The map is also a scoreboard

We're gamifying policy progress. Each year, the states that climb the rankings the most earn A Tripping Ball — a night that lands where the momentum is. It turns a map into a movement: locals get a real reason to improve their state, and we show up to celebrate them when they do.
