What we do

Trip on ideas, not substances.

We bring the science of psychedelics and the mind to the public through upscale live events, original content, and grounded education — hosted by comedian and science communicator Shane Mauss.

The Flagship

A Tripping Ball — a dress-to-impress evening of comedy, science, a candlelit dinner, live music, and a headline conversation with a genuine legend. A touring event that lands in a new city and returns.

The Engine

Every event is captured multi-camera and becomes social, a podcast, and a documentary — a growing library that compounds our audience city to city.
