Legal ≠ Safe

The most harmful drugs in America are the legal, normalized ones. If we ranked drugs by the damage they actually do, the picture would look nothing like the law.

Ranked by actual harm

A landmark 2010 study in The Lancet scored 20 drugs on 16 measures of harm — to the user and to others. Alcohol came out the single most harmful drug of all. Psychedelics landed at the very bottom.

Why we put this next to the policy map

None of this is an argument that any drug is harmless. It's an argument for honesty — for a drug policy that tracks real-world harm instead of history and habit. When the two legal recreational drugs top the harm charts and the safest substances sit in the same legal category as heroin, the labels aren't protecting anyone. They're just out of date.

That's the whole premise of The Elevated States Project: bring the science to the public, plainly, and let people see the gap between what's dangerous and what's illegal for themselves. The policy Atlas tracks where the law is finally starting to catch up.
