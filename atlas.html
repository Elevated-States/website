Eli Lilly places a $3.8B bet on psychedelics — the field's largest pharma investment yet.

Colorado

Regulated psilocybin healing centers are now operational under the state's Natural Medicine program.

North Carolina

Lawmakers advance a $4.2M university psychedelic-research bill (Duke / UNC), alongside UNC's $26.9M DARPA program.

The state funds $5M for ibogaine and $5M for psilocybin research — among the largest public commitments yet.

Research

FDA

Regulators continue fast-tracking psychedelic therapies, with multiple Phase 3 programs in progress.
