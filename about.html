Trip on ideas,

The Elevated States Project brings the science of psychedelics and the mind to the public — honestly, entertainingly, and unforgettably.

The mission

The trusted, science-first voice in the middle.

The field is having its biggest moment ever, but the conversation is crowded with hype on one side and stigma on the other. We're building the voice that can make you laugh, learn something true, and think for yourself. We do it three ways: science literacy (turning curiosity into real understanding), harm reduction (modeling a responsible, informed culture), and honest advocacy (holding the excitement and the evidence at the same time).

"Psychedelic culture is growing up — and we want to see it elevated, from hype to wonder."
