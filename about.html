About the Project

Grounded science, made joyful.

The mission

Trip on ideas, not substances.

The Elevated States Project brings the science of psychedelics and the mind to the public — honestly, entertainingly, and unforgettably. The field is having its biggest moment ever, but the conversation is crowded with hype on one side and stigma on the other. We're building the trusted, science-first voice in the middle: one that can make you laugh, learn something true, and think for yourself.

"Psychedelic culture is growing up — and we want to see it elevated, from hype to wonder."

We do that three ways: science literacy (turning curiosity into real understanding), harm reduction (modeling a responsible, informed culture — it discourages use), and honest advocacy (holding the excitement and the evidence at the same time).

The flywheel

Get the Ball to make the trip to you.

Our Policy Atlas ranks every state on how open it is — and tracks who's climbing. Each year, the state that rises the most earns A Tripping Ball: a night of science, comedy, and celebration that lands where the momentum is. It turns a map into a movement — locals have a real reason to improve their state, and we go celebrate them when they do.

The host

Shane Mauss

Shane is a touring comedian and science communicator who has spent years making the science of the mind funny, accessible, and human — on stage, on television, and through his long-running science podcast. A Tripping Ball is the room he always wanted to build: where the smartest ideas in the field meet great company and a genuinely good night out.

In partnership with The Pearl Institute

We're proud to launch alongside The Pearl Institute, a North Carolina nonprofit advancing psychedelic science through clinical care, education, and research. Their education mission and our events are a natural fit — and their partnership grounds everything we do in real, responsible practice.
